Privacy Policy

How M2 Solutions collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy explains how M2 Solutions Ltd ("M2 Solutions", "we", "us", "our") collects and uses personal data when you visit m2solutions.co.uk or contact us through this website.

M2 Solutions Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration number ZB428775. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

1. Who we are

M2 Solutions Ltd is a business transformation consultancy registered in England and Wales (Company No. 10295130). Our registered office is St James House, 65 Mere Green Road, Sutton Coldfield, West Midlands, England, B75 5BY.

You can contact us about data protection matters at: info@m2solutions.co.uk

2. What data we collect and why

Contact form submissions

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your company name
  • Your email address
  • The contents of your message

We use this information to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis for processing this data is legitimate interests — specifically, responding to business enquiries made to us.

Form submissions are stored securely by Netlify, our website hosting provider, and are also delivered to our email inbox. We retain enquiry data for as long as is reasonably necessary to manage our business relationship with you, and will delete it on request.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Cloudflare Analytics is cookieless and privacy-preserving by design — it does not track individuals across websites, does not set cookies, and does not use fingerprinting techniques. The data it collects (page views, referral sources, countries, browser types) is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Because no personal data is collected through Cloudflare Analytics, no consent is required for its use under UK GDPR or PECR.

Google Fonts

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font, Google's servers log your IP address. This is a standard part of how web fonts operate. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not control this processing and it is governed by Google's own terms.

3. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own. Cloudflare Analytics is explicitly cookieless. Google Fonts may cause your browser to cache font files locally, but this is not a cookie.

No consent banner is displayed on this site because no consent-requiring cookies or trackers are used.

4. How we share your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers, and only to the extent necessary to operate this website:

  • Netlify — website hosting and contact form storage (United States; Netlify participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework)
  • Cloudflare — website delivery, DNS, and privacy-preserving analytics
  • Google — font delivery via Google Fonts
  • Microsoft — email delivery via Microsoft 365 (enquiries submitted through the contact form are received in our Microsoft 365 inbox)

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@m2solutions.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

6. Data security

This website is served over HTTPS. Contact form data is transmitted securely and stored by Netlify in accordance with their security practices. We take reasonable steps to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact

For any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at info@m2solutions.co.uk.
